# Daily launch checklist (4–5 hours)

## First 30 minutes — prepare
- [ ] Confirm WhatsApp number in `src/config.js` and test the link.
- [ ] Run the website locally and test all three demo routes on mobile and desktop.
- [ ] Replace demo labels / links before showing a client; clearly identify them as concept demos.

## 60 minutes — research 15–20 prospects
- [ ] Find local businesses through public business listings, Instagram pages, referrals and walk-ins.
- [ ] Prioritise businesses with no website or a specific visible website problem.
- [ ] Record only publicly available business contact details in the tracker.
- [ ] Write one genuine reason why a website improvement may help each prospect.

## 60 minutes — outreach
- [ ] Send 10–15 personalised, relevant messages (not bulk spam).
- [ ] Keep the message short and ask permission to share a demo.
- [ ] Respect “no”, opt-outs and platform messaging rules.

## 60–90 minutes — demos and paid delivery
- [ ] Prepare one tailored preview only for a genuinely interested prospect.
- [ ] Confirm scope, fee, timeline, content responsibilities and revision count in writing.
- [ ] Collect the agreed advance before starting custom client work.

## 20–30 minutes — follow-up and review
- [ ] Follow up once after 2–3 business days where appropriate.
- [ ] Update the tracker and schedule next actions.
- [ ] Review what message / customer category produced real replies.

## Weekly targets (activity, not guaranteed outcomes)
- 60–80 researched prospects
- 40–60 personalised first contacts
- 10–15 respectful follow-ups
- 3–5 relevant sample previews for interested prospects
- Aim for 1 paid project; adjust based on actual response rates.
