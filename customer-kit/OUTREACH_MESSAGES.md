# MonoForge Studio — customer acquisition messages

Replace bracketed fields and only send messages that are relevant to the recipient. Do not mass-spam or claim to have audited a website unless you actually have.

## 1. First WhatsApp message — business without a website

Hello [Name], I’m [Your Name] from MonoForge Studio. I came across [Business Name] and wanted to ask whether you have considered a simple website where customers can see your services, timings, location and contact details in one place.

I build mobile-friendly websites for local businesses. I can share a short sample relevant to your business and a clear fixed quote before you decide. Would that be useful?

## 2. First WhatsApp message — existing website

Hello [Name], I’m [Your Name] from MonoForge Studio. I had a look at [Business Name]'s online presence and noticed [specific, genuine observation — e.g. the phone number is difficult to find on mobile / the menu PDF is hard to read on a phone].

I help local businesses improve their websites and mobile enquiry experience. If you are considering an update, I can suggest a small, clearly scoped improvement and quote it before starting. Would you like me to send an example?

## 3. Coaching centre version

Hello [Name], I’m [Your Name] from MonoForge Studio. I create simple websites for coaching and tuition centres, with course details, class timings, faculty information, location and an admission enquiry button.

I have a sample education website I can show you. If you are interested, I can prepare a quote based on the pages and features you need. May I share the demo?

## 4. Restaurant / cafe version

Hello [Name], I’m [Your Name] from MonoForge Studio. I build mobile-friendly websites for restaurants and cafes, with menus, location, opening hours and a WhatsApp reservation/contact option.

I have a sample restaurant layout available to preview. Would you be open to seeing it? I can quote based on your actual menu and requirements.

## 5. Follow-up after 2–3 business days

Hi [Name], just following up on my message about a website for [Business Name]. No pressure if it is not a priority right now. If useful, I can send a short sample and a simple breakdown of what would be included. Thank you.

## 6. Reply when someone asks “How much?”

Thanks for asking. A one-page website starts at ₹1,999, and a small multi-page business website starts at ₹3,999. The final quote depends on the content, number of pages and features. Domain, hosting, paid services and copywriting are separate unless listed in the quote. If you share your requirements, I’ll confirm the exact scope and price before any work begins.

## 7. Reply when someone says yes to a demo

Great, thank you. Here is the sample: [DEMO LINK]. It is a concept demo, so the sample business details are placeholders. For your website I would use your real business name, services, photos, location and contact details. If you like this direction, I can send a short scope and quote.

## 8. Ask for project details

To quote accurately, could you share:
- Business name and what you offer
- Main goal of the website (calls, WhatsApp enquiries, bookings, information, etc.)
- Pages you need
- Your logo, photos and written content (if available)
- Contact number, address and social links to display
- Any example website you like
- Your preferred launch date

## 9. Payment and project-start message

Thanks for confirming. The agreed scope is [pages/features], the total project fee is ₹[amount], and the target delivery date is [date] after all required content is received. This includes [number] revision round(s). Domain, hosting and paid third-party services are excluded / [state any included items]. To schedule the work, the advance is ₹[amount] (50%). The balance is due after you review and approve the preview, before final handover. Please reply “I approve this scope” if everything is correct.

## 10. Delivery / testimonial request

Hi [Name], the agreed website updates are ready for your review: [preview link]. Please check the pages, spelling, contact details and mobile layout. Once you confirm the work matches the agreed scope and the final balance is settled, I’ll complete the handover. If you’re happy with the result, a short honest review would really help my new studio.
